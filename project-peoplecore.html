Project Overview

InsightTera-PeopleCore HRIS is a workforce management platform designed to centralize employee records, attendance, leave, scheduling, onboarding and offboarding, approvals, reporting, and administrative controls in one connected environment.

01

The Challenge

HR operations become difficult to manage when employee information, attendance records, leave requests, schedules, lifecycle activities, approvals, and reports are spread across separate files and processes. This limits visibility and increases the effort required to keep workforce information consistent.

02

The Solution

Designed a structured HRIS that brings employee information and workforce workflows into one system. The platform connects employee records with attendance, leave, schedules, lifecycle cases, approvals, reporting, administrative settings, and integration controls.

Core Capabilities

  • Employee profile and employment record management
  • Attendance monitoring and calendar views
  • Leave balances, requests, and approvals
  • Schedule templates and employee assignments
  • Onboarding and offboarding lifecycle workflows
  • Centralized approval inbox and request handling
  • Workforce reporting and management insights
  • Work calendar, policies, roles, and administration
  • Google Workspace, biometric, and service integrations
