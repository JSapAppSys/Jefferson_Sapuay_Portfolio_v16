Project Overview

A centralized IT Asset Management System designed to manage hardware assets across the complete lifecycle—from registration and assignment through transfer, return, maintenance, QR identification, audit history, and retirement.

01

The Challenge

Spreadsheet-based asset tracking can result in inventory discrepancies, unclear accountability, incomplete ownership history, weak maintenance records, lost equipment, and difficult audits. The challenge is not only knowing what assets exist, but understanding where they are, who owns them, what condition they are in, and what happened throughout their lifecycle.

02

The Solution

Designed an ITAM workflow that centralizes hardware inventory and connects each asset to assignment, transfer, return, maintenance, QR tagging, audit history, warranty, location, and lifecycle status information.

Core Capabilities

  • Centralized hardware asset registry
  • Detailed asset profile, specifications, warranty, and ownership
  • Assignment and issuance workflows
  • Transfer and reassignment controls
  • Return and retrieval process
  • Maintenance and repair history
  • QR code generation and asset tagging
  • Audit trail and activity history
  • Lifecycle status from registration through retirement
