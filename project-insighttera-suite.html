Project Overview

Insight Tera Suite is a custom integrated ERP designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises streamline internal operations by bringing business processes, operational data, workflows, and management visibility into one connected platform.

01

The Challenge

SMEs often depend on spreadsheets, disconnected applications, and manual workflows across departments. That fragmentation makes it difficult to maintain a consistent view of operations, coordinate work, and understand what is happening across finance, inventory, warehouse activities, and other business functions.

02

The Solution

Designed and developed an integrated business platform that brings multiple operational modules into one suite. The system demonstrates a shared platform approach across analytics, accounting, chart of accounts, inventory, and warehouse/location management.

Core Capabilities

  • Integrated suite-level analytics and operational visibility
  • Accounting dashboard and financial monitoring
  • Structured chart of accounts
  • Inventory and stock management foundation
  • Warehouse, floor, aisle, and location setup
  • Shared platform identity and access
  • Modular architecture for additional business functions
  • Business-focused interface and workflow design
